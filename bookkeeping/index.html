FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to already have QuickBooks Online?

No. If you're not on QuickBooks yet, we'll help you choose the right plan and get everything set up from scratch. If you're already using it, we can start with a cleanup or jump straight into monthly care.

How far behind can my books be?

We regularly take on catch-up projects covering several months to multiple years. The consultation includes an honest estimate of the time and cost involved before any work starts.

Do you file taxes?

We focus on bookkeeping — categorization, reconciliation, and financial reporting. We prepare clean, accurate books that hand off smoothly to your CPA or tax preparer.

What software do you use?

QuickBooks Online exclusively. If you're currently on desktop software or spreadsheets, migration to QuickBooks Online can be part of your setup engagement.

How does pricing actually work?

Every engagement starts with a free consultation. You'll get a flat, fixed price for setup or cleanup work, and a predictable monthly rate for ongoing bookkeeping — based on your transaction volume and number of accounts.
