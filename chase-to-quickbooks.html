Importing into QuickBooks Desktop 2021

  1. Open your company file, then choose File > Utilities > Import > Web Connect Files.
  2. Select the downloaded .QBO file.
  3. When asked, choose Use an existing QuickBooks account and pick your Chase bank or card account. (First time only; QuickBooks remembers it.)
  4. Open Banking > Bank Feeds > Bank Feeds Center to review, match and add the transactions.
Each transaction gets a stable ID, so importing overlapping date ranges will not create duplicates.
